Vanish is a lightweight, modern browser extension written in TypeScript that automatically hides the mouse pointer during video playback on Disney+, YouTube, and custom streaming sites after a customizable idle delay.

Features:
- **Disney+ Native Support**: Custom Web Component & Shadow DOM support.
- **Configurable Idle Delay**: Adjust the timer before the pointer vanishes (0.5s to 10s).
- **Custom Domain Manager**: Support for any streaming platform or local media server.
- **Playback & Fullscreen Mode**: Hide strictly in fullscreen or on any active video page.
- **Multi-Browser Support**: Available for Chrome, Brave, Edge, and Firefox.
